# Case study: Fenwick IT

*Fictional document for the pipeline skill demo.*

Fenwick IT is a managed service provider with 41 clients and 96 protected devices.

## Situation

Fenwick's technicians started every morning by working through failed backup alerts from their previous vendor. Many alerts were false alarms, but each one had to be checked. The owner wanted to switch but worried a migration would tie up the team for months.

## What the migration looks like

Fenwick moved client by client. Each client ran on the old system and the new one side by side for two weeks, and the old one was retired only after a test restore passed. The first six clients moved in the first two weeks. A Pellmoor engineer joined a weekly call until the last client was done.

## Before and after

| Measure | Before | After migration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technician hours per month on backup alerts | 58 | 14 |
| False-alarm alerts per week | about 120 | 22 |
| Clients with a tested restore in the last 90 days | 9 of 41 | 41 of 41 |

Fenwick tracked technician hours on backup alerts by client, which showed that a handful of clients produced most of the noise.

## Rollout

All 41 clients were migrated in nine weeks. No client lost a backup day during the move.
