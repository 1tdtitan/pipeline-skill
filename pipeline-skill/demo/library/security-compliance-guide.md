# Security and compliance guide

*Fictional document for the pipeline skill demo.*

For MSP security and compliance leads evaluating Pellmoor.

## Where data is stored

Backups are stored in two United States regions selected during setup. The partner chooses the regions for each client. Access is limited to named partner administrators, and every access is logged.

## Encryption and immutability

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Backup copies are immutable for a retention period the partner sets, which means they cannot be changed or deleted during that period.

## BAA and compliance documents

Pellmoor signs a business associate agreement (BAA) with partners who serve healthcare clients. The BAA is signed electronically during onboarding. The document pack that comes with it includes an independent audit report summary, a data flow diagram, and a subprocessor list.

## Completing a security review

Pellmoor returns a completed standard security questionnaire on request. Custom questionnaires are handled by the partner's account manager together with Pellmoor's legal team.
