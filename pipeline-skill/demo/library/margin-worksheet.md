# Backup margin worksheet

*Fictional document for the pipeline skill demo.*

A fill-in worksheet for estimating what backup costs an MSP to deliver.

## Inputs

- Protected devices
- Monthly vendor cost per device
- Technician hours per month spent on backup alerts and restores
- Loaded hourly cost of a technician
- Monthly price charged to clients

## How to use it

Enter your own figures. The worksheet does not include benchmarks. Bring the completed sheet to a review with your Pellmoor account manager.
